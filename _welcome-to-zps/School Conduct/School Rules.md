<summary><strong>1. School Attire and Appearance</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p><strong>1.1 School Uniforms</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Pupils are to wear the prescribed school uniform and modification to the
uniform is not allowed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The school uniform should be worn when attending school and representing
the school at functions outside school.&nbsp; This includes activities
held during the school vacation.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>For Girls:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>All buttons on the blouse to be fastened.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pinafore must be worn at all times except during PE lessons.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pinafore should be knee length.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>1.2 Shoes and Socks</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Pupils have to wear white canvas shoes.&nbsp; Shoelaces must be white.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>No leather shoes, high-cut shoes, sport shoes, slippers or sandals are
allowed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Socks must be plain white and cover the ankles. Ankle socks are not allowed.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p><strong>1.3 PE Attire</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>School PE T-shirts and shorts are to be worn on PE days or when required.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>PE T-shirts must be tucked in at all times.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>For girls, pinafore must be worn over PE attire.
<br>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>1.4 Hair for Boys</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Male pupils must be neat in appearance, clean shaven and no facial hair
is allowed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Hair must not reach the eyebrows, ears or shirt collar.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Hair should not be highly cropped, permed, coloured or tinted.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Fancy haircuts with shaven line designs are strictly prohibited.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Hair must be short and neatly combed.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>1.5 Hair for Girls</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Hairstyle should be simple and neat.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Girls with hair beyond the collar must tie it up neatly with a black elastic
band.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Fringe should be kept short or pulled back neatly with a black hairpin
or hairband.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Hair should not be permed, coloured or tinted.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p><strong>1.6 Jewellery and Ornaments</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Jewellery or ornaments are not allowed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Boys are not allowed to wear earrings or ear studs of any kind.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Girls who have their ears pierced should only use one simple ear stud
on each ear.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Tinted glasses and contact lenses are not allowed.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>1.7 Nails and Make-up</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Fingernails must be kept short and clean.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Nail varnish is not allowed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Putting on of make-up is not allowed.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>2. Attendance and Punctuality</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Pupils who do not report to school by 7.30 am are considered late.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>National Anthem and Pledge-taking ceremony will be held at 7.30 am on
Monday and at 7.40 am on Tuesday to Friday.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Absence from school must be accompanied by a medical certificate and/or
where appropriate, a letter of explanation from parents/guardian.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>3. Pledge-taking and Singing of National Anthem</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Pupils who are Singapore citizens must sing the National Anthem and take
the Pledge.&nbsp; Pupils will take the Pledge with the right fist placed
over the heart.&nbsp;</p>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>4. Leaving School Early</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Pupils are expected to remain in the school premises until they are officially
dismissed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If a pupil is unwell during curriculum time, he/she may report to the
General Office and rest at the sickbay with the permission of the teacher.&nbsp;
The pupil should return to class once he/she is well.&nbsp; Otherwise,
the pupil should go home and rest.&nbsp; The admin staff in the General
Office will inform members of the family to fetch the pupil home from school.&nbsp;
A form authorising the pupil to leave early must be filled at the General
Office by the member of the family.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If a pupil needs to leave school early for valid reasons during curriculum
time, a letter will have to be given to the Principal for approval through
the form teacher prior to that day.&nbsp; A form authorising the pupil
to leave early must be filled at the General Office by the member of the
family on the day that the child needs to leave early.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If the pupil is representing the school in a game, a competition, or an
official function, he/she may leave his/her class only at the time stated
in the consent form.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>5. Leaving School During Lunch Break</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Pupils must not leave the school premises after school while waiting for
the start of the next activity, e.g. remedial, enrichment classes or CCA,
unless parents’ requests have been made in writing and their teachers have
been informed.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>6. Academic Discipline</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Pupils are to get ready for lessons with the relevant textbooks and worksheets,
etc. on the desks.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pupils must be attentive during lessons.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>All work given must be completed on time by the pupils.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Cheating in tests and exams is a serious offence.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Personal belongings which are not required for school use are not allowed
in school.&nbsp; These include card games, toys, pets and electronic devices
such as mobile phones, smart watches and handheld games.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pupils are not to use personal digital devices, including smartphones,
during school hours including recess, CCA and after-school programmes (e.g.
supplementary/ enrichment / remedial lessons).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pupils who bring such devices are to put them in lockers or their school
bags (in the absence of lockers) before school start time. They can retrieve
the devices after school ends. Pupils should bear responsibility for safekeeping
of devices.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>7. General Conduct</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Pupils are not allowed to eat in class except during the stipulated snack
break. Drinking of plain water is allowed throughout the day.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Desks and chairs must not be defaced.&nbsp; It is a serious offence to
deface any school property.&nbsp;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>No pupil should remain in the classroom or return to the classroom during
recess unless accompanied by a teacher.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Permission must be obtained from the teacher before the pupil can leave
the classroom. Pupils who need to leave the classroom during the changeover
of periods should get the permission of the teacher of the next period.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Movement to and from the classroom should be brisk and orderly. Pupils
should also move quietly in order not to distract other pupils having their
lessons.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pupils are not allowed to enter any classrooms without the teacher’s permission.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pupils should not run or play in the canteen.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pupils are to queue up to purchase food.&nbsp; Food and drinks are to
be consumed in the <u>canteen only</u>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pupils should return all used crockery to the receptacles provided by
the vendors.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pupils should help to clear the table in the canteen after use.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Silence must be maintained at all times while waiting for the assembly
programme to start.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pupils are not allowed to record photos, videos, audios, etc. within the
school or during learning journeys except when authorised by staff member
as a part of curriculum or special projects. Pupils are also not allowed
to upload media content portraying any aspect of the school onto social
media except when authorised by staff member as a part of curriculum or
special projects.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pupils are not allowed to send disparaging remarks, unsubstantiated rumours,
embarrassing pictures and videos, etc. via email or post them on social
networking sites. This is considered as Cyberbullying.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
</details>

</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>All pupils are not allowed to have in their possession any weapon. They
are also not allowed to bring any weapon-like item which can be used or

</li>
</ul>
